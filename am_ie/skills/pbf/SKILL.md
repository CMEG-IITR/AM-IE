# Powder Bed Fusion (PBF)

## Synonyms

- **Scan Speed**: scanning speed, laser scan velocity, hatch velocity, laser speed
- **Layer Thickness**: build layer height, powder layer thickness, slice thickness
- **Laser Power**: irradiation power, beam power

## Extraction notes

- Laser power and scan speed are very often reported together in one clause (e.g. '200 W and 1000 mm/s') — check for both even if only one is named explicitly nearby.
- Hatch spacing and layer thickness are frequently listed in the same sentence as a parameter set.
- Watch for energy density (e.g. J/mm^3) reported instead of raw laser power/speed — if only energy density is given, note it but do not back-calculate power/speed.
