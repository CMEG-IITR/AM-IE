# Stereolithography (VPP-SLA)

## Taxonomy sections

- Process Parameters — VAT-SLA
- Feedstock Properties — Resin

## Synonyms

- **Scan Speed**: laser scan speed, galvo scan speed

## Extraction notes

- Laser-based SLA reports scan speed (mm/s) in addition to exposure time — DLP/MSLA subtypes do not have this parameter since they expose a full layer at once.
